Welcome to Cricket Ad Muter

✓ You're all set. Open a live match and ads will be muted automatically.

Ad breaks go quiet during live cricket on JioHotstar and SonyLIV, and the sound comes back the moment play resumes. Three quick steps:

  1. 1

    Pick where you watch

    Chrome will ask to let the extension work on the sites you pick. It needs that to notice when an ad starts. It works on no other site, and nothing is collected or sent anywhere. You can add the other one later from the extension's settings.

  2. 2

    Pin it to your toolbar

    Click the puzzle-piece icon 🧩 at the top right of Chrome, then the pin next to Cricket Ad Muter. You'll see a live countdown on it during ads.

  3. 3

    Watch a match

    Open any live match. Ads are handled automatically, with a badge on the video while one plays. Want to hear an ad, or mute one that slipped through? Use the badge's button, or press Alt+Shift+M. Press Alt+Shift+H to pause.

Cricket Ad Muter doesn't block or skip ads; it only controls your tab's volume. Not affiliated with JioStar, JioHotstar, Sony Pictures Networks India or SonyLIV.
